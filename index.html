THE PROBLEM
AI Has Changed Strategy. Credibility Hasn’t Caught Up.

Founders are increasingly using AI tools to generate business cases, market analyses, and strategy decks.
The speed and polish are impressive.
But investors are seeing the same failure modes repeatedly:

• Markets that look large but don’t adopt
• Cost curves that ignore physics or manufacturing reality
• Timelines that collapse under scale-up scrutiny
• Narratives that sound right but don’t survive questions

The result is a growing credibility gap between AI-generated strategy and investor decision-making — even when the underlying technology is strong. 

THE PROOFPOINT APPROACH
AI Proposes. Experience Disposes.

AI is used to explore landscapes, scenarios, and narratives quickly.
Human expertise is applied to validate what actually matters.

Every engagement includes explicit review of the assumptions investors challenge most:
• Who really buys — and why
• How costs actually scale
• What breaks during manufacturing or deployment
• Where timelines slip in the real world
• What capital intensity looks like under stress

The result is strategy that is faster than traditional consulting, more rigorous than AI alone, and designed to stand up in the room where capital is committed.
